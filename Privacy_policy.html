Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

At PiPath, we respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your personal information secure.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an interest form or register through our ads or website, we may collect contact details such as your full name, email address, phone number, and grade/education level.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is strictly used for contacting you regarding PiPath tutoring services and PiPath bootcamps. Specifically, we use your details to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and answer questions about our programs.
  • Provide schedule updates, curriculum information, and registration details for upcoming bootcamps.
  • Follow up with personalized tutoring recommendations.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal contact information with third parties or outside marketers. Your details remain private and are solely used by the PiPath team to communicate with you.

4. Opt-Out & Contact Us

If you no longer wish to receive communications regarding PiPath bootcamps or tutoring, or if you would like your information removed from our records, please reach out to us directly.
