PiPath SAT Math Bootcamp Terms and Policies

Effective date: June 15, 2026

Course Format

The SAT Math Bootcamp includes live online instruction, assigned practice, and related course materials as described on the SAT Math Bootcamp enrollment page.

Student Participation

Students are expected to attend sessions on time, participate respectfully, and complete assigned practice to receive the full benefit of the course.

Enrollment

Enrollment in PiPath's SAT Math Bootcamp reserves one of a limited number of seats. A student’s seat is considered reserved once payment has been submitted.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Because seats are limited and course preparation begins after enrollment, all payments for PiPath's SAT Math Bootcamp are final and non-refundable once submitted, except where required by law, if PiPath cancels the bootcamp, or if PiPath is unable to provide the course as described.

If PiPath cancels the bootcamp or cannot provide the course as described, families will receive either a full refund or the option to transfer enrollment to a future course.

If a student can no longer attend after payment has been submitted, the family may contact PiPath before the course begins to ask about available transfer options. Transfers or credits are not guaranteed and are subject to course availability.

Contact

Questions about these terms may be sent to pipathmath@gmail.com.
